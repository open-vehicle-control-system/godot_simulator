## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Design: one compute compositor effect per stereo camera (sensors/camera_effect.gd, shaders/camera.comp), attached only when the pack names an effect, working on the view's colour in scene light before tone mapping: a metering pass (16x9 samples, geometric mean, gain kept in a 1x1 texture), the camera pass (lens, shutter, vignetting, noise, gain) into a scratch texture, and a copy back. Pack fields under vehicle.stereo: distortion {k1,k2,p1,p2,k3}, vignetting, noise {read, shot}, exposure {target, speed_per_s, max_stops}, motion_blur {shutter_s}.

Distortion: the view is drawn as a wider pinhole (camera fov and Viewport.scaling_3d_scale raised by the lens's overscan, at most 2), resampled through the inverse of OpenCV's model (12 fixed-point steps, the same in LensDistortion and distortion.inc), then scaled to the image's size, so the image and its intrinsics keep their size. calib.json carries distortion_coefficients [k1,k2,p1,p2,k3]. The ground truth camera draws the same wider view and its effect remaps depth and labels through the lens with nearest sampling, so the maps are those of the distorted left image; disparity stays fx*baseline/depth (rectified).

Motion blur uses the renderer's velocity buffer (needs_motion_vectors), which holds every surface's motion, the camera's included (measured against a reprojection from depth: equal); the sky is reprojected by the camera's turn. Exposure adaptation is the effect's own rather than Godot's CameraAttributes auto exposure: it is deterministic and its unit is the pack's.

Options not taken: black corners instead of overscan; applying noise after tone mapping.

Tests: test_lens_distortion.gd (OpenCV's formula on known values, undistortion, overscan, folding), test_vehicle_packs.gd (fields, off when absent, ranges), test_stereo.gd (calibration, overscan of the cameras); rendered: tests/rendered/test_camera_effects.gd (a target is shown within 1 px of where OpenCV's model projects it and the ground truth depth is the target's there; vignetting ratio; noise deviation and independence of the cameras; exposure reaching its target; an edge smeared by the motion during the shutter).

Measured (Spa, 120 km/h, 1280x720 pair, RX 7800 XT, GPU time over all viewports): pinhole 9.3 ms; vignetting, noise, exposure +0.1 ms each; motion blur +0.2 ms; distortion k1 -0.17 k2 0.03 (overscan 1.3) +1.3 ms; all +1.4 ms. Sensor noise makes PNGs slower to encode: recording in real time then skips frames (113 of 141 kept); the recorder now keeps four threads free and reports skipped frames. Captures: build/captures/174, 175. No shipped pack sets these fields (vehicle packs are generated by tools/pack_experiment.py); captures use a copy of ovcs1 under build/packs_camera.
